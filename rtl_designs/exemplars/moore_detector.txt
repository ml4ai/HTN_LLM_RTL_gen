Module name: seen_110
Inputs: clock, reset_n (active-low reset, asynchronous), d
Output: hit
Implement a Moore finite state machine that detects the bit sequence 110 on d. After the third bit of the sequence has been received the machine is in a state DONE in which hit is high; hit is low in every other state. The states are WAIT, GOT1, GOT11 and DONE. A new occurrence may begin with bits of the previous one.
