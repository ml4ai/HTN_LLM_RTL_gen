Module ramp generates a triangle waveform on a 4-bit output level. It has inputs clock and rst_n (active-low asynchronous reset). An internal one-bit register dir holds the direction: when dir is 0, level increases by 1 each clock; when level reaches 15, dir becomes 1 and level stays at 15 for that cycle. When dir is 1, level decreases by 1 each clock; when level reaches 0, dir becomes 0 and level stays at 0 for that cycle. Reset clears dir and level to 0.
